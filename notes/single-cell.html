SAVE: Gene-block-as-token (a different granularity)

SAVE breaks the "one gene = one token" assumption entirely. Each gene's NCBI Summary text is embedded with an LLM (text-embedding-ada-002 → 1,536-d). The G gene embeddings are partitioned into L balanced non-overlapping blocks of K=3,200 genes by iterative Optimal Transport. The cell × gene matrix Y ∈ ℝ^{N×G} is reshaped into a block tensor X ∈ ℝ^{N×L×K}, and attention is performed across the L block tokens.

  • Advantages: attention cost drops from O(G²) to O(L²) — Table 8 reports 191× speedup over flat per-gene attention; preserves high-level functional/pathway structure rather than relying on the model to discover it
  • Limitation: block construction depends on quality of LLM-derived embeddings; poorly characterized genes may be misgrouped
  • Used together with adaLN condition injection + Flow Matching, not gene-token autoregression
