Projects

  • Multi-omics Biomarker Discovery in Kidney Xenotransplantation

    An integrative multi-omics study to identify clinical biomarkers of graft survival and rejection in pig-to-primate kidney xenotransplantation. I integrate bulk RNA-seq, single-nucleus RNA-seq (snRNA-seq), 10x Visium spatial transcriptomics, and proteomics to dissect the immune response, macrophage polarization, and chronic fibrosis that drive long-term outcomes.

    RNA-seq · snRNA-seq · 10x Visium · Proteomics · Multi-omic analysis

  • Large-scale scRNA-seq Atlas of Brain Organoids

    Constructing a large-scale single-cell RNA-seq atlas of brain organoids and quantifying how faithfully they recapitulate primary human tissue. I apply deep-learning approaches — spatial deconvolution that maps single cells onto tissue, and reference-based single-cell integration via transfer learning with conditional variational autoencoders — to benchmark organoid fidelity and transfer annotations across datasets.

    Brain organoid · scRNA-seq · Spatial deconvolution · Reference-based integration · Transfer learning

  • In silico Modelling of Cell-state Dynamics

    Modelling cellular state dynamics in silico with stochastic differential equation (SDE) frameworks. I formulate and solve stochastic problems to capture noise-driven state transitions and cell trajectories, bridging dynamical-systems theory with single-cell measurements.

    SDE · Stochastic modelling · Cell dynamics

  • Quantifying Cellular Abnormality from scRNA-seq

    Proposing a method to quantify cellular abnormality at single-cell resolution from scRNA-seq. Using reference-based cell-type probability matrices, the approach scores how far each cell deviates from normal reference states, giving an interpretable measure of abnormality.

    scRNA-seq · Cell-type probability · Abnormality scoring
